# BlueLine Recruiting — funnel design brief

- **Design read:** CDL-A drivers (80% on a phone, often in a cab) deciding in 8 seconds whether this is a real recruiter or a lead farm. Register: plain, specific, confident. Zero hype.
- **Concept spine:** "Blue hour departure." The page is one pre-dawn pull-out: a new truck leaving the yard as the sky opens. Every chapter is a closer look at the same truck, the same road, the same promise.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema (scroll-scrub hero; the engine owns scroll-to-time, surrounding motion is transform-only).
- **Locked palette (user's existing brand, overrides the dark+blue ban):** ground #0A1324 (live site navy), panel #101C33, line #22314D, ink #F3F6FB, muted #A9B6CC, accent #1A6EF4 (live site button blue), accent-hover #3D86FF. Defense: the brand already is navy + BlueLine blue; the funnel must look like the same company the ad came from.
- **Locked type:** Barlow Condensed 700/800 (display: the condensed industrial voice of trucking signage, matches the existing all-caps BLUELINE wordmark) + Barlow 400/500/600 (body). Sans only. No serif.
- **Logo:** replica of the live wordmark: BLUELINE in black-weight caps with a blue strike-line and arrowhead, RECRUITING letterspaced beneath. Same mark on every page, sticky bar and footer.
- **Animation mode: animated-website** (Jay asked for the Wahr Homes style scroll-driven video hero).
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One 15 s continuous film, split at frame boundaries into 3 chapter clips so the copy changes while the move never cuts.
- **Journey:**
  1. 0-5 s, low on wet asphalt, headlight comes on, camera rises. H1 "Brand new truck. 71¢ a mile. Home when you say." Body: 3 months CDL-A experience is enough. Tags: $10K a month · Paid weekly · 100% free.
  2. 5-10 s, orbit past the cab, truck starts rolling. H2 "Apply once. We do the applying." Body: no ten forms on ten sites. Tags: Vetted carriers · Number never resold.
  3. 10-15 s, aerial pull-back, dawn opens. H2 "A real recruiter calls in 5 minutes." Body: one human, your name, your home-time ask.
- **World grammar:** one brand-new white Class 8 tractor, plain white dry van, no logos. Pre-dawn blue hour, wet road, low fog. Navy/cool-blue grade, single electric-blue accent in the headlight flare, no orange grade, locked exposure, slow steady crane-orbit-pullback, no cuts, no text.
- **Mobile framing:** truck stays in the center-safe 60% of frame; mobile clip crops via object-position center; copy sits over the dark upper/lower thirds. Mobile encodes at 720p.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop clips ≤32 MiB total, mobile ≤16 MiB total.
- **Section plan (after the journey):** (1) Quick apply form, split 50/50 [form family]; (2) Pick your lane, 3 offer cards that pre-fill the quiz [card row]; (3) Four driver objections answered, 2-col zigzag [zigzag]; (4) How it works, 3-step horizontal rail [rail]; (5) Text to apply, full-width band [band]; (6) FAQ accordion [accordion]; (7) Final CTA + footer. Eyebrow budget: 2.
- **Asset plan:** film + 6 posters (exact first frames), OG/cover image, favicon mark (inline SVG wordmark), no stock.
- **CTA inventory:** Primary "See if I qualify" (solid blue, arrow, lift on hover) → /apply. "Apply in 60 seconds" (form submit, full-width, blue). "Call (407) 683-5894" (outline pill, phone glyph). "Text us" (ghost with sms: deep link). Sticky mobile bar: Call · Text · Apply (three equal cells, Apply filled).
- **Claims policy:** no named carriers, no bonus dollar figures, no invented stats or reviews. Only what the ad promises and what BlueLine controls (free, 5-minute callback, weekly pay, vetted carriers).
